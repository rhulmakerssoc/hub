HOW CAN I GET MY DESIGN TO BE FABRICATED?

You can e-mail your design to makers@royalholloway.su, and tell us what colour, and material (for laser-cut only) you would like.
The files should be either an STL for 3D printing, or an SVG/DXF for laser-cutting.

Colour Options
Laser Cutting
SOLID: Black, White, Purple, Blue, Green, Red
TRANSPARENT: Clear, Light Blue, Dark Green, Red
FLUORESCENT: Orange, Yellow, Dark Blue, Red

3D Printing
PLA: Black, White, Red, Orange, Green, Blue...

(Please note that these colour options may change at any time. Please contact us for the colour options at the time.)

SOLDERING CERTIFICATION

A soldering certification session is required for all members to use the soldering irons in our electronic laboratories.
This is to ensure that all members are able to use the equipment safely and effectively.
Please request a session with the form below if you are not yet certified with us, thanks.

WHEN CAN I ACCESS THE ELECTRONICS & PC LABS?

As a member, you can access the SHILLING-1-06 electronics lab as long as there is at least a technician & a member of the committee present.
The preset lab access hours for societies are Wednesday 13:00-17:00, which will usually be our workshop times.
If you would like to use the lab equipment outside of these hours, please ask one of the committee members for the availability of the lab.
Alternatively, you can also check the detailed timetable of the lab on the RHUL Timetable System. Simply go to 'Location', select the 'Electronic Engineering' department, and 'SHILLING-1-06', then select the week(s) and "View Timetable"

*** There will be no access available during week 11-12, 19-20, 27-28 & 34-36 due to EE project deadlines. ***

WORKSHOPS
